Planning Your Visit

Astana is one of the fastest growing modern capitals in Central Asia. The city is famous for its futuristic architecture, wide boulevards, and the scenic Ishim River dividing the historic right bank from the modern administrative left bank.

Summer months bring open-air concerts, lively fountains, and riverside walks, while winter brings ice rinks, bright seasonal illuminations, and cozy spots across the city centre.
